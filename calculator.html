Score a system, item by item

This calculator runs the field worksheet, version 2. Answer what the evidence shows, mark what is untested, and it works out the capacity band and the self profile, with ranges wherever the evidence is incomplete.

Status: a proposal, not a validated instrument. Every reference score was made by one surveyor, and the bands are coarse. The band estimates how much of a particular architecture a system has; it does not measure experience. How each item is defined, and why, is on the methodology page. The earlier slider calculator (v1) is kept as the legacy calculator.

Step 0: Place it on the tiers

Answer in order. This step carries no points; it decides whether SIU is scored at all.

Step 1: Score SIU

Yes is 0.2, Partial 0.1, No 0. Sensing items are scored on their own. Integration and Unity are ladders: an item counts only if the one before it scored at least Partial, and the ladder stops at the first No. Mark untested items U; the calculator scores them both ways and reports the range. Tags (F field, L lab, N neuroanatomy, R related species) go on the record and do not change the score.

Construct rules. A receptor means a physical transducer the system carries (a camera, a microphone, a motion sensor); input handed to it already digitized counts at most Partial on S1, and channels with no transducer count at most Partial on S2. Learning counts only if it happens inside the system during its own operation. I5 does not apply: Integration is I1 to I4, divided by 0.8.

Steps 2 to 4: The three self tracks

These score rungs, not points. A track reaches a rung when an indicator for that rung is shown and the rung below is reached. An untested indicator does not raise a rung. A result from a single study or a single lab, or a test that left out a component, gives a range down to the rung below. A contested reading (one that could be behavior-reading) counts toward the upper end only. Evidence from a related species is recorded but does not set a rung.
